About

I am an Aerospace Engineering student at UC San Diego with a focus on aircraft design, flight dynamics, and computational analysis. My work spans conceptual design, aerodynamic modeling, stability analysis, and engineering optimization.

I enjoy building end-to-end analysis pipelines that connect physics-based models to real design decisions — from constraint diagrams to CMA-ES optimizers to AVL-based dynamic stability analysis.
